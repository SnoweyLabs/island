### design-3-2 — at the fly-in stretch the hole is a stadium round an ellipse (LOW, `Defect_`)

Where: `MovingLight.PlaceShapes` given the stretched width and height and the **unstretched** radius (`IslandController.UpdateMovingLight`: `w = frame.Width * StretchX`, `h = frame.Height * StretchY`, `frame.Radius`); the old drawing stretches the groups that hold the capsule's fill, the rim and the bloom with a `ScaleTransform` about the centre (`IslandView._backScale`, `_frontScale`, and the clip's hole `hole.Transform = new ScaleTransform(...)`), so the capsule and the old hole have **elliptical** corners (`rx*StretchX`, `ry*StretchY`). `RimOutline.Of` clamps the radius to half the shorter side, so a stretched ball becomes a stadium with circular ends.
Evidence: `Round3CutOutTests.Record_The_Cut_Of_A_Stretched_Ball_Against_The_Stretched_Outline` (passes, real GDI regions): ball 30 by 30 stretched by (0.864, 1.34) = 25.9 by 40.2: **at 100% 35 pixels outside the ball are cut and 24 inside show glow (outline 132 px); at 150% 44 and 51 (198 px); at 200% 116 and 72 (264 px)**; the milder stretch (1.15) 14 and 24 at 100%. Wider balls (50 by 36 and 80 by 44 before the stretch; the sizes are my assumption, UNVERIFIED how long the machine holds them: the stretch applies while the shape is under 12% of the way to capsule width and moving fast) leak 210 to 1082 pixels inside the outline (about one to two pixels deep all round, 100% to 200%) and cut 0.
`Defect_The_Cut_Of_A_Stretched_Ball_Never_Reaches_Outside_The_Stretched_Outline` (red: `116 px outside the stretched ball are cut out of the glow (the outline is about 264 px long at 200%)`). At rest the same quantity is 0 at every scale.
What Snowey would see: for the few frames of the fly-in where the ball is stretched, the glow lacks a stretch next to the ball's poles (the shadow shows instead) and shows inside it at the flanks. A few frames; the old light, which carries the stretch, draws no such gap. Round 2 (section 4, item 3) recorded that the strokes keep their width; this is the cut, which round 2 did not have.
Smallest repair: give `LightFrame` the stretch (or the elliptical radii) and make the region from `CreateRoundRectRgn(..., 2*rx, 2*ry)`, or hold the cut and show no glow inside for the frames while `StretchY != 1`. No picture changes.
