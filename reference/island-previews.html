Island — three looks

Use the buttons. Or click the page once and use the keyboard: Space show / hide · 1–5 category · ← → pick an item.

★Your pick: Liquid Glass in the size of Black Capsule

The glass, the glowing rim and the round icons of B, in the small one-row shape of A.

Watch out: the blur behind the glass only exists in this preview. Press the button to see the version Windows can do for sure.

ABlack Capsule

Solid black, like the island on an iPhone. A thin comet of light runs around the edge. Icons in one row, the picked one named beside them.

Watch out: safest to build, but it looks like most island apps already out there.

BLiquid Glass

See-through glass that picks up the colours behind it. The whole rim glows in the category colour and a soft highlight slides along it. Items as round tiles.

Watch out: here the glass is real. On Windows it can only be imitated, because an app cannot blur the desktop behind a shape that changes size. Hardest of the three.

CGlyph Console

Matte grey with a ring of small LED dots that step around the edge. Numbered list, like an instrument panel. No bounce, everything snaps.

Watch out: the least Apple-like of the three.
